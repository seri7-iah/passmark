# Design rules

Before building or changing any UI:
1. Call the designer-skill tool `load_project_context` and read DESIGN.md.
2. Call `anti_slop_checklist` and follow it.
3. Use only the fonts, colors and spacing in DESIGN.md. Do not invent new ones.
4. Do not add gradients, glow effects, floating cards, emoji icons or decorative
   shapes unless DESIGN.md asks for them.
5. Use real, specific text. No filler like "Welcome to our platform" or
   "Unlock the power of...". If you don't have real content, leave a clear
   placeholder and tell me.
6. Before finishing: open the page, take a screenshot at desktop and phone width,
   and fix anything broken, cut off, unreadable or misaligned.
7. Keep changes small. Tell me what you changed and why.
